Our own product · In development

A free showcase for young builders.

Council Computing built Launchpad, a place where students and young founders post the websites, apps, games, and startups they've made. Visitors browse a gallery, filter by category, search, and upvote their favorites, no account needed.

Launching soon. The screenshots on this page are from the working build.
The problem

Student projects have nowhere to be seen

Young builders make impressive things, then share them in a group chat and they disappear.

Big launch sites bury them

On sites built for funded startups, a student's first app competes with companies that have marketing teams, and sinks.

No place to point people

For a college application, a job, or just showing friends, there's no single page that says "here's what I built."

What we built

A gallery that's fun to browse and safe to run

Quick for makers to post to, easy for visitors to explore, and moderated so it stays clean.

Gallery, search & filters

Browse by category (games, web apps, AI tools, hardware, startups), search by project or maker, or hit "Surprise me" for a random pick.

Upvotes without accounts

Anyone can upvote once per project with no sign-up, and a "Top this week" view surfaces what's popular right now.

Post in a minute

Name, link, one-line pitch, a screenshot, and an optional YouTube or Loom demo video. That's it.

Review before it goes live

Every submission waits in an admin queue to be approved, rejected, or featured on the home page.

Built-in spam protection

Rate limits, a hidden spam trap for bots, and strict checks on links, videos, and uploaded images.

Made to be shared

Every project gets its own page with a rich preview, so a maker's link looks good in a text, a DM, or a post.

The real thing

Designed for the phone in your pocket

Built mobile-first, with smooth animations and a light and dark mode.

How it works

From "I made this" to the front page

1

Submit

A maker posts their project with a screenshot and a short pitch. No account required.

2

Get reviewed

An admin checks it and approves it, usually within a day.

3

Go live

It appears in the gallery with its own shareable page.

4

Get upvoted

Visitors vote, and the best projects rise to the top or get featured.

The outcome

A community product, built to run on its own

⚡

Zero friction

No sign-ups for makers or visitors, so posting and voting take seconds.

🧹

Stays clean

Moderation and spam protection are built in from day one, not bolted on later.

📣

Makers spread the word

Shareable project pages turn every maker into someone who brings in new visitors.
