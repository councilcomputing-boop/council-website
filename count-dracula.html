What's inside
Five ways to learn the count
Purpose-built drills, not a generic flashcard app — built around the same Hi-Lo system real counters use.

Classic / Peek Practice

Play simulated hands against the dealer with betting and splits — peek at your running count for a cost in Blood Drops.

Quiz Practice

The same game, but the count stays hidden — the dealer demands it of you at moments of his choosing.

Deck Math

A calculator and drill for converting a running count into a true count — the number that actually matters at the table.

Speed Drill

A full deck flashes by at speed. Keep the count in your head and answer for it at the end.

Collectible Card Backs

Earn Blood Drops from drills and daily bonuses, then spend them on chests, spins, and card-back skins in the Store.

How it works
Four steps to a real edge
1

Pick a mode

Manual, Classic/Peek, Quiz Practice, Deck Math, or Speed Drill — start wherever your count needs the most work.

2

Count as the cards fall

Track the running count using Hi-Lo values: low cards +1, middle cards 0, high cards and aces −1.

3

Convert to true count

Divide the running count by decks remaining in Deck Math to find the number that actually tells you the edge.

4

Earn Blood Drops

Drills and daily bonuses earn Blood Drops — spend them on peeks, chests, and card-back collectibles.

Get it on your phone
No app store. Just add it to your home screen.

iPhone

Must use Safari
  1. Open countdraculacards.com in Safari (not Chrome)
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
  4. Tap Add in the top right

Android

Works in Chrome
  1. Open countdraculacards.com in Chrome
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top right corner
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen
  4. Tap Add to confirm
